Cosmic Creatures

PRIVACY POLICY

Effective: 7 October 2026 · Who: Zac Haley, trading as Bazooka Zac ("we", "us") · Contact: cosmiccreaturesgame@gmail.com

The Short Version

Cosmic Creatures does not collect, sell or share personal information. The game has no accounts, no online play, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

What Stays On Your Device

The game keeps these files on your own device, in its own save folder:

  • Your saves — your current run, your collection, the codex and your progress.
  • Your settings — sound, display, language and controller choices.
  • The wizard names you type — shown on your screen and kept in your own saves.
  • Engine log files, on computers only — technical notes that help diagnose problems.

None of this is sent to us, and we cannot see it. It is yours to remove:

  • ERASE EVERYTHING, under SETTINGS on the home screen, wipes your runs, collection, progress and the wizard names you typed in.
  • Deleting the game's data removes everything, settings and names included. On a phone or tablet, deleting the app does this. On a computer, the save folder stays after the game is uninstalled until you delete it.

Your device or the store you use may back up or sync your saves between your own devices. That copy is held by them, under their own privacy policy, not by us.

Playing Together

Pass & Play and VS AI duels are played on one device. They use no network connection and share nothing with anyone.

No Third-Party Services

The game contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and no third-party code that collects data. It makes no network connections. Where the game offers to open this policy in your browser, that page is fetched by your own browser, and the website's host may record the visit as any website does.

If You Write To Us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to answer you, keep it only as long as that takes, and delete it whenever you ask. Our email provider stores those messages for us.

Children

Cosmic Creatures collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Nothing typed into the game leaves the device. If a child writes to us, we use the message only to reply and delete it on request from the child or a parent.

Platforms And Stores

The store or platform you got the game from, and the device you play on, run their own services — your account, purchases, achievements, cloud saves, crash reports and the like — under their own privacy policies. They may share with us sales totals and, where you allowed it, anonymous crash or usage reports. We use these only to look after the game.

Your Rights

Wherever you live — including under the GDPR in the EU and UK and the CCPA in California — you may ask what personal information we hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted. The game itself gives us none. For anything you have emailed us, write to the address above and we will answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes — for example, if online play is added in a future update — the new version will appear in the game, with a new effective date, before anything new is collected.
