Cosmic Creatures

PRIVACY POLICY

Effective: 2026-09-27 · Who: Zac Haley, publishing as Bazooka Zac ("we") · Contact: cosmiccreaturesgame@gmail.com

The Short Version

Cosmic Creatures collects no personal information and sends nothing to us. The game has no online play, no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What Stays On Your Device

The game keeps these files on your own device, in its private save folder:

Your saves — your current run, your collection, the codex and your progress.

Your settings — sound, display, language and controller choices.

The wizard name you type — used only on your screen and in your own saves.

Engine log files — technical notes the game engine writes on computers, to help with problems. They stay on your device unless you choose to send them to us.

None of this leaves your device. We cannot see it, and you can remove all of it at any time: SETTINGS on the home screen offers ERASE EVERYTHING, and uninstalling the game removes it too. Your platform may copy saves between your own devices (for example Steam Cloud or a device backup); that copy is kept by the platform, not by us.

Playing Together

Pass & Play and VS AI duels are played on one device. They use no network connection and share nothing with anyone.

No Third-Party Services

The game contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software, and no third-party software development kits that collect data. It makes no network connections while you play.

Children

Cosmic Creatures collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Nothing a child types into the game leaves the device.

Platforms

If you get or play the game through Steam, the App Store or another store, that platform's own privacy policy governs the data it collects (your account, purchases, achievements, crash reports and the like). We receive none of it except what a platform may share with every developer in anonymous, aggregate form (such as total sales).

Your Rights

Because we hold no information about you, there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. Everything the game knows is on your device and under your control. If you have a question, write to the address above.

Changes

If this policy changes — for example, if online play is added in a future update — the new version will appear in the game with a new effective date before anything new is collected.
